04 / WHAT IS REALLY EVOLVING?

The rules, not a picture

↗

Growth

A local tip follows weighted resource cues, keeps its previous direction, avoids obstacles, explores randomly, and may branch. Every segment costs length. Reaching a resource earns fitness.

Inheritance

Six rule families compete. Each carries seven continuous traits. Fitness-based tournament selection retains a champion and mutates descendants; a same-budget neutral population evolves without selection.

Evidence boundary

This is an abstract evolutionary-developmental sandbox, not simulated plant physiology or proof of neuron-like intelligence. The unseen test changes each generation, so inspect trends across seeds, not one lucky winner.
